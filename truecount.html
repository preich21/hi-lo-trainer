True count = running count ÷ decks left in the shoe. A running count of +6 means a lot with 1½ decks left (true count +4) and little with 5 left (about +1). Your bets and strategy changes follow the true count.

You estimate the decks left from the discard tray: see how many decks are in the tray and subtract from the shoe size. Counters work to the nearest half deck, so every tray here shows a whole or half deck.
