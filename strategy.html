Red badges count your wrong answers on that hand. Never take insurance or even money: without a count it always loses. That changes once you count, at a true count of +3 or higher.

Basic strategy depends slightly on the table rules. Set them to match the casino you'll play in; the drill and the chart follow immediately. The defaults are the usual European shoe game.

The strategy assumes 4–8 decks and that you may double on any first two cards. It comes from the standard multi-deck charts, with the three European no-hole-card changes: hit 11 against a 10, hit 8,8 against a 10 or Ace, and hit A,A against an Ace.
