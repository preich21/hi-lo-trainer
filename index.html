Limits

These limits decide whether a correct round counts as casino-ready, workable or too slow. Any wrong answer always counts as miscounted, whatever the time.

The presets are deliberate. Counting down a full deck in about 25 seconds (0.5 s per card) is the usual training standard for counters. Pairs are stricter because high and low cards cancel out. Flash is measured per hand including your tap, close to the time you get at a real table when the dealer flips and sweeps cards. If you're casino-ready at all three, you're well prepared for real card counting. Change them only if you want to start easier and tighten them later.
